About Me

Hi, I'm Zondra.

Zondra
Founder, Inbox Aide

I started Inbox Aide because I kept seeing small businesses lose customers over something completely fixable — slow, disorganized replies. Not bad products. Not bad service. Just a communication gap nobody had diagnosed.

I don't sell a one-size-fits-all package. Every business's audit is different, so my recommendation is different — sometimes it's a couple of small fixes you can make yourself, sometimes it's my templates, and sometimes it's full inbox management, which I only offer to 1–2 clients at a time so each one gets my full attention.

Everything starts the same way: you send me screenshots of your real conversations, I diagnose exactly what's happening, and I tell you the right next step — honestly, even if that step doesn't involve me at all.

I never log into your accounts, and I never ask for passwords or access. It's always just screenshots, on your terms.
