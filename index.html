About Me

Hi, I'm Zondra.

Founder, Inbox Aide

I started Inbox Aide because I kept seeing small businesses lose customers over something completely fixable — slow, disorganized replies. Not bad products. Not bad service. Just a messy inbox.

I'm not running an agency. I work with businesses directly, one inbox at a time, from my phone, wherever I am. I only take on 1–2 management clients at a time — because I'd rather do a handful of inboxes brilliantly than a hundred badly.

Everything else I've built — my checklist, my guide, my app — exists so you can fix things yourself too, at your own pace, even if you never become a client.
