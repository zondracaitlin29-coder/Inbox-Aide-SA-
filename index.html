Answering the same questions again and again is completely normal when you run a small business. Often the information is already there. It just isn’t easy for customers to find, or the wording leaves them unsure what to do next.

Small changes can make a real difference: answering the actual question first, keeping messages short enough to scan, and ending with a clear next step. That makes things easier for your customers and for you, with less repeated typing.

  • Messages that feel too long, unclear or hard to scan
  • A WhatsApp Business profile that’s incomplete or out of date
  • Catalogue descriptions that don’t explain the product or service well
  • Wanting to sound professional without sounding cold or unlike yourself
